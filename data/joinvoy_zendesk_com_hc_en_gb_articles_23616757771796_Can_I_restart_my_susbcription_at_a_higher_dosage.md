Yes, for Wegovy and Mounjaro, you can start on a higher dosage if you've already begun your treatment with another provider. Here's what you need to know:

## Starting at a Higher Dose

1. During our [eligibility questions](https://www.joinvoy.com/n/weight-loss/quiz/1 "https://www.joinvoy.com/n/weight-loss/quiz/1"), we'll ask if you are currently taking any GLP-1 medication
2. Select 'Yes' and confirm the type of medication you're taking
3. Confirm the last dose you injected and when you last took your medication
4. Continue through the eligibility questions until you reach the checkout
5. Select the dose you'd like to order and checkout - this will start your subscription

## Providing Evidence of Your Current Prescription

After placing your order, you'll be asked to complete 3 tasks on your online account

1. ID
2. A full-length photo of yourself for the purpose of this subscription
3. evidence of your current medication

If you're unable to complete one of the tasks, you can message our Clinical Team on the [Support](https://www.joinvoy.com/account/support) page under 'Speak to Medical Team' for guidance. You can also upload a photo to your [messages](https://www.joinvoy.com/account/messages) by clicking on the paperclip icon within the messaging space.

## The evidence should include

- Your legal name
- Name of the medication
- The dosage
- The date the prescription was approved

Acceptable forms of evidence include:

- Screenshot of your NHS record
- Consultant letter
- Photo of the prescription label on your medication box
- Prescription confirmation email from an online provider

We're here to ensure you receive the most appropriate treatment for your needs. If you have any questions about starting on a higher dosage, please don't hesitate to reach out to our Medical Team through your account messages.
