Due to prescribing regulations, we're currently only licensed to prescribe and deliver to addresses within the UK.

## Reasons for UK-Only Service

1. **Prescribing Regulations**: Current prescribing regulations restrict us from providing our services internationally.

2. **Legal Compliance**: To ensure we're operating within the bounds of the law and providing the safest possible service, we must limit our operations to the UK.


Travelling Abroad?

If you're currently signed up to Voy and you will be travelling outside of the UK for your next delivery, we can amend your next order date so that your treatment arrives before or after your trip.

Reach out to our Customer Care Team by emailing [help@joinvoy.com](mailto:help@joinvoy.com) or using the livechat feature on your online account with the dates of your travel and your preferred order date and we'll update this for you.

You might also find this FAQ helpful: [How can I amend my next order date?](https://joinvoy.zendesk.com/hc/en-gb/articles/20199306207124--How-can-I-amend-my-next-order-date)

We're constantly evaluating opportunities to expand our services. While we can't make any promises, we're always looking for ways to help more people on their weight loss journeys.
