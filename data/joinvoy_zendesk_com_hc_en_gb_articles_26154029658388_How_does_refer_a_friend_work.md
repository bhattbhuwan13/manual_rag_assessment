We're delighted that you're considering sharing Voy with the people you know! Our refer-a-friend programme is our way of saying thank you for spreading the word about your weight loss journey and our service. Here's how it works:

### For your friend

They'll receive a discount up to £45 on their first order.

### For you

You'll earn a discount up to on your next order based on how many friends you refer.

Go to our [Refer a Friend](https://www.joinvoy.com/account/refer) page to check up to how much you can earn.

## How to refer a friend

1. Log into your Voy account
2. Go to your [Refer a Friend](https://www.joinvoy.com/account/refer "https://www.joinvoy.com/account/refer") page (top right corner of your account)
3. Click the orange 'copy' button next to your referral link
4. Share this link with your friend (paste it in a message)

Your friend needs to use this link when placing their order for the discount to apply.

## Tracking your discounts

Check your [Refer a Friend page](https://www.joinvoy.com/account/refer "https://www.joinvoy.com/account/refer") to see the discount for your next order.

We're excited to have you share the Voy experience with your friends. Together, we can create a community of support on your weight loss journey!

\*You will receive the discount on your next order once the person you referred **has placed an order and it has been approved**. If they only sign up without placing an order, you will not receive the discount.

## Other Discounts

We do not currently offer any other discounts such as NHS discounts, Key Worker discounts or Student discounts.
